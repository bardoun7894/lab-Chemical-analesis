{# The note under a measurement grid when there is nothing to judge against. Until 2026-08-27 the CCM wall thickness, cement and coating standards came from Settings > Dimension Standards, keyed by DN, and this file rendered the whole Standard/Deviation block. They now come from the product's Application table for the standard the order was built to, and the grids render three editable Min/Nominal/Max boxes instead. What survived the change is the part that mattered: an empty band must say it is empty. A grid that silently judges nothing looks exactly like a grid where everything passed. A nominal with no Min and no Max is the second case. The deviation is still worth showing, but no pass/fail verdict can be given — under exact equality every reading would be red, which tells the operator nothing.
